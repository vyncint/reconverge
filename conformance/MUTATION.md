## Reading the numbers

- **wrapbar** — detection at default confidence requires the witness
interpreter to replay a concrete hang; sites whose path to the barrier
crosses branches on values the interpreter honestly cannot know stay at
`warning` (visible with `--strict`). Both numbers are the product. A
wrap that lands on a barrier upstream *already* keeps under divergent
control cannot exceed the unmutated baseline and counts as undetected —
attribution never double-claims a pre-existing finding (at this pin,
every `--strict` miss is one of those found-in-the-wild sites).
- **delbar** — a deleted barrier is a data race, which static divergence
analysis cannot see by design (races are outside the decidable slice;
the witness replays only *found* divergence bugs). Expected recall 0;
the row is published anyway, and doubles as a precision invariant:
removing a barrier must not conjure findings.
- **wrapcol** — same mechanics as wrapbar, over the collectives the
dialect classifies (`shfl*_sync`, `ballot/any/all_sync`). Sites hidden
behind upstream helper wrappers (`match_*_sync`, typed `shuffle_*`) are
outside the v1 surface and counted under site accounting below.
Promotion additionally needs a mask the analysis can evaluate; upstream
writes masks as named consts, which `rustc_public` cannot evaluate at
the pin, and an unevaluable mask is never witness-promoted — it could
be the correct guarded partial-warp idiom.
- **shrinkmask** — a shrunk full mask at a *convergent* call site is a
mask-lane mismatch only under launch shapes the static engine does not
model (RC002 v1 checks convergence; it does not do mask arithmetic
against launch shapes). Expected recall 0 in v1; the class stays in
the corpus so the gap is public and any improvement shows up here.
- **mutslice** — RC003 is a syntactic `deny`; expected recall 100% of
compiling mutants. Swaps whose kernel body needs `DisjointSlice`-only
API do not compile and are pruned (counted below).
